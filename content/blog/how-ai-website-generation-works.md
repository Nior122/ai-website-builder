When you describe your business in a sentence or two, the AI Website Builder
turns that description into a complete website — pages, sections, copy, and
imagery. This post explains what happens between *you pressing Generate* and
*seeing a rendered site*.

## 1. Understanding the description

The first step is turning your free-text description into a structured model.
The AI identifies the business type and industry (restaurant, SaaS, law firm,
agency, and many more) and selects an appropriate page set: a restaurant gets
*home, menu, about, reservations, gallery, contact*, while a SaaS startup gets
*home, features, pricing, about, blog, contact*.

## 2. Generating sections per page

Each page is composed of sections — hero, features, testimonials, gallery,
contact, footer, etc. For every page in the set, the AI plans which sections
belong, in what order, and fills them with copy tailored to your business:

- A **hero** with a headline and primary call-to-action.
- **Features** or **services** describing what you offer.
- A **gallery** or **showcase** where the content type warrants it.
- A **contact** section with placeholders you can wire up later.

## 3. Choosing a theme

Every industry has a default theme — *luxury* for fine dining, *modern* for
SaaS, *corporate* for legal practices, *creative* for agencies. The theme
controls color palette, typography pairing, spacing rhythm, and accent
treatments so the generated site already feels on-brand before you touch
anything.

## 4. The visual editor

Once generated, the site opens in the visual editor where you can drag
sections to reorder them, tweak copy inline, swap themes, adjust per-section
styles (padding, background, radius, shadow), and add or remove sections.
Nothing here is locked behind code — every value is a field in the editor.

## 5. One-click deploy

When you are happy with the result, you deploy. The studio can publish to
Vercel, Netlify, or Cloudflare, or you can export the raw code and host it
yourself. Generated sites are static-first and ship fast by default.

---

The whole loop — from description to a live, editable site — takes seconds.
That speed is the point: you spend your time refining, not scaffolding.
